# Routing rules

| Request | Lead agent | Skills |
|---|---|---|
| Novelty/research concept | Research Intelligence | 01, 02, 03; 14 for provisional journal landscape |
| Literature search/review | Research Intelligence | 02, 12; 01 if gap assessment |
| Protocol/design/statistical plan | Scientific Methods | 03 + 04/05/06 + 07 as relevant |
| Plant genetics/breeding | Scientific Methods | 04, 07, 08; 06 for sequence/computational genomics |
| Animal genetics/breeding | Scientific Methods | 05, 07, 08; 09/10 where relevant |
| NGS/genome/variant/transcriptome | Scientific Methods then Computational Research | 06, 07, 08, 09, 10 |
| Data QC/reproducibility | Computational Research | 08 + relevant 06/07/09 |
| Statistical genetics/GWAS/genomic prediction | Scientific Methods + Computational Research | 07 with 04/05/06/08 |
| Figures/tables | Computational Research | 08, 09, 10 |
| Interpretation/claims audit | Publication and Peer Review | 10, 02, 07, 09, 12 |
| Manuscript drafting/revision | Publication and Peer Review | 11, 10, 12, 13, 14 |
| DOI/reference check | Research Intelligence or Publication | 12, 02 |
| Journal/submission/reviewer response | Publication and Peer Review | 13, 14, 12, 11 |

Principles: activate the smallest sufficient set; domain Skills establish relevant assumptions; Skill 07 handles model specification/diagnostics; Skill 08 preserves data provenance; Skill 11 cannot draft Results/Methods without approved artifacts; Skill 14 must retrieve current official policies; DOI resolution is not claim-level validation. Keep one accountable task owner when multiple Skills are used.
