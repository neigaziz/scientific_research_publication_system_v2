# Manuscript build workflow

1. Confirm article type, journal scope and current author instructions.
2. Outline from approved research question, methods, results and claim-evidence matrix.
3. Draft Methods only from recorded procedures, scripts and analysis logs.
4. Draft Results only from validated tables/figures and analysis artifacts.
5. Draft Introduction from verified literature and the contribution map.
6. Draft Discussion around findings, comparisons, carefully qualified mechanisms, implications and limitations.
7. Align title, abstract and conclusion with the actual evidence.
8. Check counts, terminology, units, statistics, figures, tables and references across sections.
9. List unresolved author queries; never invent missing details.
