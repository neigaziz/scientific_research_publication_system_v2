# Plant breeding and genetics — method-selection notes

Confirm crop, target trait, population, mating/design structure, phenotyping environment, genome assembly/build, marker platform and breeding objective. For multi-environment trials assess genotype-by-environment interaction, trial connectedness, replication, spatial field variation and environmental covariates. For GWAS examine population structure, relatedness, marker quality, phenotype definition, multiple testing and independent validation. For genomic prediction define training/validation populations, avoid leakage, report validation design and uncertainty, and compare baselines. For QTL/linkage studies document population type, map quality and segregation assumptions. Do not infer a causal gene from an associated marker without appropriate fine-mapping or functional evidence. Record genome and annotation versions.
