# Animal breeding and genetics — method-selection notes

Clarify species, breed, population, breeding objective, phenotype definition, record frequency, repeated-record structure, pedigree depth/completeness, genotypes, contemporary groups, management effects and target genetic parameter. For longitudinal traits distinguish observed records from derived indicators; document curve fitting and residual derivation; test sensitivity to trait definitions and filtering; consider parity/lactation stage, herd-year-season, permanent environment and repeated records. Choose pedigree/genomic relationship structures according to available data and study design. For heritability and genetic correlations specify model, scale, effects, covariance structure, estimator, uncertainty and diagnostics. Check pedigree connectedness, genotype quality, selection/ascertainment bias, convergence and biological interpretation.
